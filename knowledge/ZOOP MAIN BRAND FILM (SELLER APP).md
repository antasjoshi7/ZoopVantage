Zoop is a social commerce platform that helps fashion resellers/ boutique owners/ retailers/ wholesalers in India.

Currently 90% of sellers are female, mostly existing live sellers (they have been selling on Facebook live, or YouTube live since Covid time) and some new to live retailers & boutique owners.

The target persona is female (35-55 yrs).

Profit and financial independence are key motivators. They unlock a new channel to sell to audience across India, without learning websites + ads + more complex ecomm listings. Super simple rooted in Indian culture 1 to 1 customer facing relation but via live showcasing to hundreds of people in real time as if they are physically in their shop. The convienience of ecomm with fun, trust & advantages of offline physical shopping.

HOOK FOR MULTISTREAM REEL

Kya aap bhi 2 - 3 phone se Live kar karke pareshan hogye ho? Har phone mein alag message padhna, sabko alag alag reply dena!

Agar aap Zoop App se Live jaate hain, toh ab aapko teen phono ki zarurat nahi. Aap apne teeno platforms - Facebook / Youtube aur Instagram, ke saare comments ek hi jagah padh payenge. Kyuki hamara Multistream feature khas isi problem ko solve karta hai. Aur Multistream feature istemal karke aapke Views, Reach aur audience bhi ekdum tezi se badhne lagte hain. Hamare kayi sellers ki sales Zoop App se Live jaane ke baad, 3-5X tak hogyi hai.

HOOK FOR AI CATALOGUING REEL

Kya aap bhi woh Live Seller ho, Jo ghanto apne products ki photo kheechke cataloguing karte ho? Cataloguing. Website. Sabkuch manage karna mushkil nahi hojata?

Zoop App ke saath cataloguing ki tension ko bolo bye. Hamara AI Cataloguing feature, smartly aapke Live se, aapke hi products ko Cataloguing karke aapke, Online storefront pe upload kar deta hai. Ab ghanto baith ke apne product ki photo kheenchke upload karne ki koi zarurat nahi.

HOOK FOR SMART REEL FEATURE

Kya aap bhi ek aise Live Seller ho, Jisko Insta pe content banana, Live Sell karna, sab kuch manage karna padta hai? / Kya aap ek aise Live Seller ho jisko Insta aur Youtube pe organically apni following badhani hai?

Pehle jo Product Reels banane ki tension thi, ab woh bhi gyi! Kyuki Aapke Live se, automatically, high quality, reels edit karke, Zoop ka Smart Reel feature, aapke Social Media platforms pe upload bhi kar deta hai.

Toh ab aap sirf Live jao aur Social Media presence aur following ki tension bhi humpe chodd do! Zoop ke saath badhao khud ki following!

HOOK FOR GIVEAWAY FEATURE

Agar aap ek Live Seller ho, toh aap giveaway toh karte hi hoge aur aapko Giveaway ki taaaqat pata hi hogi. / Aapki audience aapki lives sirf tab tak dekhegi jab tak woh engaging hogi, ye toh aapko pata hi hoga!

Zoop App istemal karke aap apni Lives ko aur interesting aur engaging bhi bana sakte hain. Kyuki hamara Giveaway feature ye ensure karta hai ki aapki audience aapki lives zyada se zyada der tak dekhe. Jissey FB aur YouTube’s jaise platforms ko la gta hai ki engagement achi hai, toh unka algorithm automatically aapki lives pe organically aur audience bhej deta hai.

HOOK FOR BEAUTY FILTER

Aapki Live kitni sunder dikhti hai directly affect karti hai aapki Live ke views. Zyada sunder Live matlab zyada views.

Zoop App ke Beauty Filters and Stickers istemal karke aapki Lives ban jaati hain aur bhi beautiful aur Visually appealing. Jisse aapko extra compliments milte hain, and aapki Lives ki quality overall badh jaati hai.

HOOK FOR AI PRACTISE FEATURE

Socho? Aapki Lives pe aapka koi saathi hota? Jo aapko Live ke har kadam pe saapki madad karta?

Itne sab ke baad bhi hamare features khatam nahi hote! Hamara ‘AI Practice Mode feature’, aapko sikhata hai, ki ek acha Live Seller banne ke liye kya chizein zaruri hain? Audience ko engaged rakhne ki tips deta hai, background improve karne ke suggestions, lighting ko improve karne ke tareeke batata hai!

Itne saare features poore India mein kisi aur Live Selling app ke paas nahi hain! Simple shabdon mein, Zoop App matlab Live Sellers ke liye Extra Views & Extra Sales. Apni agli Live Zoop se kijiye aur khud farak dekhiye! Download karne ke liye abhi click karein!
