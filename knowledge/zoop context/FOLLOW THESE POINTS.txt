While Editing Remember:
The story needs to have one thread all the way through.
Don’t introduce multiple ideas.
Don’t make too many points, that distract the viewer from the ‘MAIN’ intention. 
At the end of finishing a Video, ask yourself:
WHO AM I TALKING TO?
WHAT ARE THEY LEAVING WITH ? i.e VALUE
CAN I CHOP ANY PART OF THE REEL, SOMETHING THAT’S JUST NOISE.
SOMETHING THAT’S NOT ADDING ANYTHING, JUST MAKING IT LONG!
