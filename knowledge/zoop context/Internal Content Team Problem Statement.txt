Internal Content Team Problem Statement

Zoop needs to educate potential users about the value of live shopping, market the Buyer App effectively, and build trust with target audiences — especially Hindi-speaking women in tier 2 and tier 3 cities who are deal hunters and live-stream viewers.
Right now, awareness of live commerce as a trusted and entertaining way to shop is low, and people do not yet understand why they should download and use the Zoop Buyer App. To achieve product-market fit and growth, we need to drive a massive increase in app downloads and long-term engagement.
To solve this, we must:
* Educate people on what Zoop is, how live shopping works, and why live beats traditional e-commerce.

* Build trust by conveying authenticity, quality, and community value.

* Market the app with compelling messaging that resonates emotionally and practically with the target audience.

* Get a crazy number of downloads by learning from proven strategies companies have used to drive app installs.

* Do deep research into the best acquisition tactics — including performance ads, organic formats, virality loops, influencer and creator integrations, referral mechanics, and cultural hooks.

* Explore different markets (regional languages, city tiers, interest groups) and different content formats (short video, livestream clips, testimonials, tutorials, social proof) to maximize reach and relevance.

The core challenge is to transform unaware browsers into engaged users by crafting content that educates, inspires trust, and motivates immediate action — downloading and using the Zoop Buyer App.
