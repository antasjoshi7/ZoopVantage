 Zoop is a live commerce platform that enables sellers to sell products through live video streams. Sellers go live, demonstrate products in real time, interact with viewers via chat, and convert live audiences into buyers.
What does Zoop do?
 Zoop combines content, commerce, and community by making shopping interactive, entertaining, and trust-driven. It helps sellers build credibility and helps buyers make confident purchase decisions through live interaction.
Seller App
 The Zoop Seller App is built for sellers to host live streams, showcase products, engage with viewers, manage listings, and sell directly during live sessions.
Buyer App
 The Zoop Buyer App is a dedicated app for buyers to discover live sellers, watch live shopping streams, interact in real time, and purchase products instantly during the live.

 The Buyer App is needed to create a focused, high-retention experience for buyers.
